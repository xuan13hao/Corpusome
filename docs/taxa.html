Taxa Across Body Sites
Prevalence and mean relative abundance of every genus (16S tier) and species (shotgun tier) across body sites — computed directly from the relative-abundance tables, no modelling. Prevalence = fraction of samples in a body site where the taxon is detected (>0); mean relative abundance is averaged over all samples in that body site.
