How to Cite
Please cite Corpusome in your publications if it contributed to your research.
Dataset DOI
🔗 Zenodo DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22150633
APA Citation — Dataset
Hao, X. (2026). Corpusome, a cross-body-site human microbiome corpus for representation learning [Data set]. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22150633
APA — Data Descriptor Paper
